I've Been Reading

I feel like most years, I give myself a New Years resolution of finishing one book per month. Some years I struggle to meet that goal, whereas some years, I read way more than that. This year I feel like I've been reading quite a lot. Right now I'm reading book 4 of the Dungeon Crawler Carl series. I don't want to get into spoilers, but it's a fun book. You know how sometimes you hear so much hype around a piece of media that when you finally get around to enjoying it, there's no way for it to live up to the expectations? There's almost a part of you that wants it to be bad. Well, fortunately or unfortunately this series is just as fun and enjoyable as everyone has hyped it up to be.

Before I jumped into this series, I read 'Kill The Beast' by Serra Swift, which was another really fun and enjoyable little romantasy book. If you're looking for something light and easy, I highly recommend it. It makes for a nice weekend read.

Anyways, that's all I really have to say about that for the moment. Reading is fun, and we should all do it more.

Hey Chicken

It's been a minute.
I hope you're doing well.
I haven't thought about you in a while,
But you came rushing back
Into my thoughts the other day
When I was playing a card game,
And I got assigned a sheep token.
Can you believe it's been sixteen years?

Last night I dreamt about you.
We caught up and I told you that I missed you.
I had a chance again to kiss you,
But even in my dream
It went about the same as last time.
Do you remember?
Walking through the art gallery?
Sitting in my car?
Or in the back of the movie theater?
That palpable unspoken tension?
I thought we'd have more time.
But you were impatient.
You left to that Christian school
and got pregnant
with the very first guy you laid with.
I was only slightly devastated.

I had a great time at your wedding.
I hope that no one there could see
I was really dressed in green.
It's the only time dancing
has felt like closure.
The true end to a chapter.
And I couldn't have been happier-
For you.
And I couldn't have been sadder.
And then I moved and lost your number.
I'm sorry I haven't tried
to reach out in all these years.
I didn't want to be a bother.
How is your daughter?
And how is her father?

I just found our old message thread.
Do you remember how we used to exchange
'I love you's like greetings?
Every conversation,
Planning our next meeting.
I've been beating myself up
for how we grew apart.
I could've been Uncle Sheep,
But I couldn't set aside my feelings.

I hope you're happy.
And I hope that if you ever think of me,
You still think of me fondly.

With love,
Sheep
