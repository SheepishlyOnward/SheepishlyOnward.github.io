About

Hey, I'm Sean. This website is my small way of protesting against big social media companies. I enjoy making things and doing things, but I struggle to share updates with the people I care about. Every time I open Facebook or Instagram I feel yucky, so this is my solution!

Among many other things, I am a dog trainer living and working in West Los Angeles. I have too many hobbies. I am sometimes embarrased by the immense breadth, and relatively shallow depth of my hobbies, some of which include bouldering, photography, racquetball, hiking, music, video games, computer bullshit, eating, reading, crocheting, painting, TV, and the list keeps growing endlessly!

Find me on Instagram (where I never post) @WSeanMiller
